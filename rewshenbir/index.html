Rewşenbîr
Zanîn · Çand · Pêşkeftin

A mini-app inside PezkuwiChain · Social

Rewşenbîr is a desk, not a stage. It connects Kurdish writers, journalists, scientists and poets to readers who pay to read them — directly, without an algorithm or an intermediary. It looks like a journal, not a marketplace: it never asks a reader to perform; it asks a writer to write and a reader to subscribe.

Dignified

No streaks, no vanity counts, no “trending.” Follower counts are small metadata, never a headline number.

Native to PezkuwiChain

The same dark ground, the same tiki system, the same wallet signatures you already use. It opens from the Social section of the PezkuwiChain app.

Honest about sovereignty

Creator status cannot be revoked by validators. And if your seed phrase is compromised, the platform tells you up front that it cannot save you.

In preparation
Rewşenbîr is being built. It will open inside the PezkuwiChain app. Until then, you can read Kurdish writers on Dijital Kurdistan News.
